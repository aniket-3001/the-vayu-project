**The problem.** Delhi has plenty of air-quality numbers, and none of them tells a parent what to do. One city figure cannot see one street. The nearest monitor may be kilometres from the school, and the reading may be hours old.

**What it does.** The Vayu Project is a free web app with nothing to install. Try it without login (it plays seven real past days), or create an account so your places and family follow you to any phone or laptop. You add the places you care about: Home, a school, a park. Each card shows the air from the nearest monitor with a fresh reading, and says which monitor, how far it is and how old the reading is. Then it says what to do: walk, mask, windows, whether a school assembly should be inside. A reading over six hours old gives no advice. Where there is no monitor, the number is labelled an Estimate. The Delhi pollution stage (GRAP) is one order for all of NCR; the app shows which band each place's own air is in, as my estimate, and points to the official order. A small measuring unit I built (ESP32-S3 and a laser particle sensor, over Bluetooth) shows your room's air beside the air outside.

**Who it is for.** Delhi families, schools and housing societies.

**On real recorded days** (https://d3lmq1mze2gup3.cloudfront.net/#/week). On the morning after Diwali, 21 Oct 2025, Home read 500 on the US AQI scale, which is that scale's ceiling (the monitor read 980 µg/m³ of PM2.5), from a monitor 3.8 km away, and the app said: avoid going out. On 9 Nov 2025 two places four kilometres apart had very different air: on India's scale the last 24 hours averaged 434 at Home (the GRAP Stage III band) and 319 at the park (the Stage II band). GRAP itself is one order for all of Delhi-NCR (on 21 Oct it was Stage II; Stage III was invoked on 11 Nov). The app shows which band each place's own air is in, as my estimate, beside the official order and never instead of it. The recorded days play what was recorded next as the forecast, so they show the features, not forecast accuracy. It costs about $4.50 a month idle to run, and $0.0008 for an assistant question, so a school or society can host its own. 1,313 tests pass in one command.

**What I learned.** Keep the model's job small: rules decide, the AI only words, every number is checked. And read your own screenshots: mine showed AQI 500 beside the wrong stage.

**Not yet:** the unit has not been compared with a monitor, and I have not run a pilot.
