**The gap.** Delhi has plenty of air-quality data. What a parent, a school or a housing society lacks is the last step, from a number to a decision for their own place. A city figure cannot see one street, and the nearest monitor may be kilometres away and hours old.

**Who benefits.**

- **Parents** deciding whether a child walks to school. On 21 Oct 2025, the morning after Diwali, the official GRAP stage for all of Delhi-NCR was II. The nearest monitor with a fresh reading, Lodhi Road, 3.8 km from the home I used, read AQI 500 (PM2.5 980 µg/m³), and the band for that place's own air was IV, by my estimate. The app says stay in, names the monitor and its distance, and shows the official order beside its own estimate.
- **Schools** deciding whether assembly is inside or on the ground. Each school gets its own card, its own monitor and a direct answer.
- **Housing societies** deciding whether to hold an event or a children's play session outdoors.
- **Anyone curious about a room**, with the measuring unit: the room's PM2.5 beside the outside air, so a family can see whether a purifier is doing anything.

**What the recorded days show** (real archive data, played as if it were 8 am):

- **9 Nov 2025:** two places 4 km apart averaged 434 and 319 on India's scale over 24 hours, the Stage III and Stage II bands. A city number hides that gap.
- **31 Oct 2025:** the app counted 85 satellite fire detections near Home, or upwind within 300 km, in the previous 24 hours, and warned that smoke could arrive.

**Why it can spread.** It is free and needs no install. One shared deployment serves every place: about $4.50 a month idle and about $46 with every cap reached, so adding a school adds no cost.

**What is not proven.** There is no pilot and there are no users yet, so I claim no health outcome. The GRAP band is my estimate, checked against monitors but not against official orders, and the unit has not been compared with a monitor. Whether people act on the answer, and whether that helps, is the next question. The next step is one school for one term.
