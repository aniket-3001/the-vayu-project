For any "impact" or "who benefits" box.

**Who it helps.** A parent deciding whether the child walks to school. A school deciding whether assembly is inside or on the ground. A housing society deciding whether to hold an outdoor event.

**What changes for them.** They get one answer for one named place, with the monitor, its distance and the reading's age, instead of one city number. They see when they should not trust it: a reading over six hours old gives no advice, and an estimate says it is an estimate.

**Evidence it works, from real days.**

- 21 Oct 2025, the day after Diwali: Home read 500 on the US AQI scale (its ceiling; PM2.5 was 980 µg/m³) from a monitor 3.8 km away, and the app said avoid going out. The official GRAP stage that day was II for all of NCR; this one monitor was far above the city average, so the band for this place's air was IV. The app shows both ideas and says the official order counts.
- 9 Nov 2025: four kilometres apart, on India's scale the last 24 hours averaged 434 at Home (the Stage III band) and 319 at the park (the Stage II band). One city number would have hidden that gap. The official stage is one decision for all of Delhi-NCR; Stage III was invoked on 11 Nov.
- 31 Oct 2025: the alert counted 85 satellite fires in the last 24 hours, near Home or upwind, and said smoke could reach it within a day or two.
- These are replayed from public archives (OpenAQ, Open-Meteo, NASA FIRMS) in the app itself, labelled "recorded". The band is my estimate for a place's own air, never the official order. The recorded days play what was recorded next as the forecast, so they show the features, not forecast accuracy.

**Why others can use it.** It is free, and an account is optional: it only saves your places and family. It costs about $4.50 a month idle at list prices and $0.0008 an assistant question, so a school or society can run its own copy. The code is public (https://github.com/hrsh-kr/the-vayu-project) with 1,313 tests, and the app is live at https://d3lmq1mze2gup3.cloudfront.net.

**Honest limits.** I have not run a pilot, so I make no claim of users or health outcomes. The unit has not been compared with a monitor. The cleaner I built (about 191 cfm of airflow, ₹5,000 of parts) has no CADR measured.

**What would show impact next.** The unit beside a monitor for a few days, with the difference published. The smoke-decay test for the cleaner. One school using it for a term.
