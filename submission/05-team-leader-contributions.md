I built the Vayu Project solo, so every role in it is mine, with Claude (an AI coding assistant) helping with the typing; many commits carry its co-author line. I chose the problem and the rules, read and ran every change, built the hardware, and deployed and checked it on AWS myself. No change was accepted until `make prove` passed: lint, types, 1,313 tests, `cdk synth` and a bundle check.

- **Product owner.** I chose the problem (a parent has the numbers but no decision), the screens, and what the app may say. Deliverables: a written set of safety rules, and a list of the claims I refuse to make.
- **Cloud engineer.** The stack runs in my own AWS account. I put the provider keys into Secrets Manager myself, approved each deploy and checked it with the live smoke test, and replaced the OpenAQ key after it was rejected late on 10 October. Deliverables: the live app and API at https://d3lmq1mze2gup3.cloudfront.net, the CDK stack, an architecture note and a cost model priced from AWS's own price list.
- **Hardware builder.** I sourced the parts, wired the SPS30 to an ESP32-S3 over UART and flashed it. On 11 October the serial monitor showed my room at about 18 to 22 µg/m³ PM2.5, and the live site, connected over Bluetooth from Chrome on my Mac, showed them too. I also built a cleaner from a box, a fan, a filter and foam, for ₹5,000 of parts; its outlet airflow reads about 191 cfm (airflow, not CADR). Deliverables: the unit's firmware and write-up, and the cleaner's build notes.
- **Tester.** I audit my own screenshots. On 11 October one showed AQI 500 beside "GRAP Stage II": the estimate used whatever 24 hours the monitor had, not the last 24, and stretched one hour's PM10-to-PM2.5 ratio over the whole day. It is fixed and checked against the monitors' own PM10 on 24 station-days: 22 landed in the same stage as the monitor's own index and 2 had no estimate (too few recent hours). Deliverables: a learning log of what broke and how it was fixed, and a live-site checklist.

The first firmware assumed the wrong wiring, and an early draft called the sensor reference-grade. I caught both.
