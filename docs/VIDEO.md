Under three minutes, unlisted on YouTube. The cut below speaks about 320 words, which is about 2 minutes 10 seconds at a calm pace, so the pauses and cuts to the AWS console fit inside 2:55.

The first video ran 4:00, was a tour of features, never said "AWS" and had no numbers. This one is built on one moment: **the morning after Diwali, a parent asks: do we walk to school?** Everything else supports it. It names AWS, shows the hardware, shows the recorded days, states one honest limit and gives three numbers.

## Before you record

1. **The assistant's daily cap resets at midnight IST.** If you tested heavily today, the assistant shows "reached its limit" instead of a Bedrock-worded answer.
2. **Fresh browser profile.** Make a test account beforehand (it needs an email code). Phone width: DevTools device toolbar at 390 × 844.
3. **Tabs, in order.** A: the live site. B: `/#/week`. C: `docs/architecture-animated.html`. D: the AWS console in `ap-south-1`, one tab per page in the table.
4. **Console hygiene.** Never open a secret's value. The account number shows in the console header; blur it in the edit.
5. **Hardware.** Have the unit connected before you start (Bluetooth works in Chrome on a Mac only). Keep a 10-second recorded clip as a fallback.
6. **Format.** 1080p, the real clock, no edited timestamps. Record each segment as its own clip so a retake costs one clip.

## Do not say

- What the unit or a station costs (no source).
- "Accurate", "reference-grade" or "agrees with a monitor" about the unit.
- That the cleaner works, or that 191 cfm beats any purifier's CADR. It is airflow, and CADR is a different number.
- That a GRAP stage for a place is official, or that a school is closed. The app shows which band a place's air is in, as an estimate, beside the official order.
- Anything about Telegram except "built, switched off".
- That the assistant answers every question with the model. Fixed rules decide; the model words, or answers a question no rule covers from the facts it is given.
