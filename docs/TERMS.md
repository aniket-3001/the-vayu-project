## 1. What the app is

The Vayu Project shows air quality for places you choose and suggests what to do. It is for information. It is **not medical
advice**, **not an official order**, and **not a replacement for a doctor, a school or a government notice**.

## 2. The air numbers

- Numbers come from public monitors and weather models run by other organisations. Each number shows where it came from and how old it is.
  A reading older than three hours is not used to advise you.
- The AQI on a card uses the US EPA scale applied to PM2.5. A GRAP stage shown in the app is **our unofficial estimate**; the
  official orders come from the Commission for Air Quality Management and always come first.
- Advice such as how long to stay out or whether to wear a mask is our wording from the AQI bands. It is not a medical rule. If you
  have a heart or lung condition, are pregnant, or care for a young child, ask your doctor.
- Readings from your own measuring unit are from a low-cost sensor whose accuracy has not been checked against a reference monitor.

## 3. The assistant

The assistant's advice comes from fixed rules. A language model may reword it, and every number in its wording is checked against
the facts. It can make mistakes in wording. It cannot take any action. Do not use it in an emergency; call 112.
