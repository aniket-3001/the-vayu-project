the claim is not to be made. Checked against the repository and the live address on 11 October 2026.

## Claims we do not make, and why

| Claim                                                                                                                                    | Why not                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The unit is "scientific-grade", "reference-grade" or "highly accurate"                                                                   | It has never been compared with a monitor over days. A low-cost laser sensor is not a reference instrument. The app labels it "Measured by your own unit" and shows it next to the nearest monitor                                            |
| "It agrees with the official monitors"                                                                                                   | That is a result, and it needs a few days of the unit beside a monitor. Not done                                                                                                                                                              |
| "Rated for ten years in the open"                                                                                                        | The sensor is not an outdoor-rated product. "Ten years" is at most the datasheet's lifetime for continuous running: check the exact line before saying even that                                                                              |
| "A station costs ₹2,00,00,000" / "the unit costs ₹4,000"                                                                                 | No source is in the repository for either figure. Say them only with a citation and a bill of materials. The arithmetic (÷ = 5,000 boxes) is only the cost of the boxes, not of running them                                                  |
| The prototype's parts cost ₹5,000 (box free, fan ₹3,300, filter ₹1,500, foam ₹200), against ₹29,900 for a Dyson HushJet Purifier Compact | `docs/PURIFIER.md`, `docs/COMPARISON.md` (Dyson price from Business Standard and Reliance Digital). A price comparison only: not a performance claim                                                                                          |
| "Our cleaner beats the Dyson HushJet", or "325 m³/h against Dyson's 250 m³/h"                                                            | Dyson publishes a CADR; ours is airflow. CADR is always lower than airflow, so the numbers are not comparable. See `docs/COMPARISON.md`. Price can be compared once there is a real bill of materials                                         |
| "The cleaner cleans a room at 190 cfm", or any CADR figure                                                                               | 191 cfm is the **airflow** at the cleaner's outlet, read once on a vane anemometer. CADR (how fast it clears a room's particles) needs the smoke-decay test, which has not been run. Do not say "CADR 190" or "cleans X cubic metres an hour" |
| Alerts reach people                                                                                                                      | Telegram sending is built and tested against fakes; live scheduled sends are off                                                                                                                                                              |
| Phone Bluetooth works                                                                                                                    | Chrome on a computer is tested. Android Chrome has not been tried; iPhone Safari cannot                                                                                                                                                       |
| That a school is closed, or that a GRAP stage is in force                                                                                | The app says "likely", as our estimate, and points to the school's notice and the official order. The recorded days' GRAP stages are our estimate for those days, not a record of the order                                                   |
| The recorded days shows the weather "wind map"                                                                                           | The map's wind in the recorded days is the one wind recorded at central Delhi, drawn the same everywhere, and the map says so                                                                                                                 |
